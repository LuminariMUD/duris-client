# Code of Conduct

Keep this project's issues, pull requests, and other community spaces welcoming and useful.

- Treat players and contributors respectfully, including when disagreeing.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or make personal attacks.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, or private messages.
- Respect moderation decisions and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation when needed. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior.

Report misconduct using GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant content. For a repository-level concern without sensitive details, contact maintainers through an issue. Do not publish private details or repeat abusive content in a public report.
